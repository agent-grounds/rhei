# Rhei: Decide the merge policy, then apply it
**States:** shape-discussion-to-a-ruling

## Overview

The merge queue needs a policy: squash, merge commits, or something between.
Two participants argue it over rounds, a judge rules, and the ruling is
applied.

The discussion is the children of a parent that rules: each position and each
judgement is a child task, and the rounds are authored in advance.
